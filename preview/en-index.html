How valet parking works at Munich Airport

Valet parking at Munich Airport removes the need to find a parking space or move your luggage onto a shuttle bus. This meet-and-greet parking service lets you hand over your keys at the terminal and focus on your journey.

1 Reserve your valet parking online

Select your travel dates and book your preferred offer with the booking partner. Most providers offer a choice between covered parking and more affordable outdoor spaces. Your booking confirmation includes the agreed handover time, meeting point and your valet provider’s contact details.

2 Follow your booking instructions on arrival

On your departure day, drive to the confirmed meeting point to hand over your vehicle. Some providers also ask you to call around 20 to 30 minutes before you arrive. Your booking confirmation explains the exact procedure.

3 Hand over your vehicle at the meeting point

A driver will meet you and take your car to the parking space you booked. The vehicle’s condition and mileage are usually recorded at handover.

4 Go straight to check-in

The driver takes your car to your booked outdoor space or covered parking facility. You take your luggage directly to the departure area, leaving you free to concentrate on your flight.

5 Collect your vehicle after landing

After landing, follow your valet provider’s return instructions. Your car is brought to the agreed meeting point within a few minutes so you can continue your journey home without a shuttle transfer.

Munich Airport valet parking: frequently asked questions

Where do I hand over my vehicle?

Your provider gives you the exact meeting point in your booking confirmation. Depending on the valet service booked, the handover takes place in the departures area at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2. Always follow your provider’s confirmed directions to avoid delays or misunderstandings.

How much time should I allow for handover?

Allow around 10 to 15 minutes to hand over your keys at Munich Airport. A brief vehicle inspection is usually carried out. The driver then takes your car to your reserved parking space while you head to check-in or security.

Do I need to call before arriving at the airport?

A call is not always necessary because the meeting point is confirmed when you book. Some providers do ask you to call around 20 to 30 minutes before arrival to help them plan. If required, this will be stated in your booking confirmation.

Where is my car parked while I am away?

Your valet provider takes your vehicle to a nearby parking facility around Munich Airport, selected when you book. Depending on the operator, you can choose underground, covered or outdoor parking.

What happens if my return flight is delayed?

If your flight is delayed, you can contact the operator by phone. Many providers also monitor flight times and are available 24/7 to coordinate vehicle return after an unexpected late arrival. The provider’s terms govern cancellations, extra parking days and late collection for reasons within your control.

Are there surcharges for overnight handovers?

Some offers include overnight surcharges outside normal service hours. All additional charges must be clearly communicated before you complete your booking.

Can I book valet parking at short notice?

Last-minute booking is possible if your preferred provider still has availability for your dates. During holidays and busy travel weekends, booking earlier generally gives you a wider choice of offers.

What documents do I need at handover?

Your booking confirmation and car key are usually sufficient. Depending on the provider, have your vehicle registration document and driving licence available too. If the car belongs to a company or someone else, written authorisation may be required.
