Munich Airport Valet Parking Prices

Private valet parking at Munich Airport starts at around €90 to €190 for eight days. The total has two components: the valet charge for collecting and returning your car, and the parking charge for your stay. Compare prices and obtain a quote for your travel dates from the booking partner.

Compare valet parking prices in Munich

These prices include parking and the valet service charge. Your total depends on your travel dates, handover times and chosen parking type. Booking-partner prices may differ from the operator’s direct rate.

We may receive a commission if you book through a partner link. This does not increase the price you pay.

How much does the valet service cost on top of parking?

Where the service charge is shown separately, collecting and returning your car costs between €30 and €70. Parkservice Flieger charges €30 for the valet service. Munich Airport charges €35 for collection and €35 for return.

Parking charges are additional in these cases. Many private providers offer parking and valet service at a single package price.

What additional charges may apply?

Pay particular attention to your handover times when booking. Valet Service Clean charges an overnight supplement of €25 per drop-off or collection between 23:00 and 06:00. If both fall within this period, the total supplement is €50.

Extra parking days, car cleaning or EV charging can also increase the price. Check the amendment and cancellation terms for your chosen offer.

American Express valet parking in Munich: costs

With an eligible American Express Platinum Card, you can book Munich Airport’s valet service through your card benefit. American Express provides access to the benefit; Flughafen München GmbH operates the service.

The Amex PDF for Munich lists €44 for vehicle collection and return together, plus parking charges at the budget rate. Check the current prices and conditions before booking on the Amex valet parking page.
