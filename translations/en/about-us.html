About Us

We are a specialist information and comparison website for valet parking at Munich Airport. Our aim is to help travellers choose a suitable parking service and understand how it works before booking.

Valet parking relies on convenience and trust: you hand over your car at the airport, a driver takes it to a parking space, and it is returned when you arrive back. That is why we consider not only price but also parking facilities, the handover process, published security features, service hours and optional extras.

How we compare valet parking offers

Our website brings together offers from different parking operators and booking platforms, highlighting differences that a price-only comparison can overlook.

An outdoor space can be a practical, affordable option. Other travellers prioritise covered parking, flexible vehicle handover or extras such as EV charging and car care. We present the available information to help you find the offer that matches the level of convenience you want.

Where our information comes from

Our information is based on the published offer descriptions of parking operators and booking platforms. This includes services, parking features, opening hours, prices and customer reviews.

We do not currently carry out our own on-site inspections or award our own quality seals. Ratings are based on public customer reviews and identify their source. The binding prices, services and terms are always those displayed by the booking partner before you complete your reservation.

How this website is funded

Some links on this website are affiliate links. If you make a booking through one of them, we may receive a commission from the booking partner. This does not increase the price you pay.

This income helps us run the website, organise offer information and provide further content about valet parking. The services you can access directly through our website also depend on the offers available from our connected booking platforms.

Who provides the valet service?

The valet parking service at Munich Airport including vehicle collection, parking and return, is provided by the parking operator named in the offer. Your reservation is made with the booking partner shown, where you can review the binding services and contractual terms before booking.
