How safe is valet parking? Key points to consider

Safety depends on more than the parking space itself. Here are the main points to consider and the measures providers can take to make their valet service safer.

Documented vehicle handover

Record your vehicle's condition together with the driver. The handover report should note existing damage, mileage and fuel or battery level. Additional photos provide a clear reference for collection and help resolve questions later.

Insurance for transfer journeys

A member of staff drives your car between the terminal and the parking facility. Even on a short journey, accidents or damage cannot be ruled out. The provider should therefore have insurance that covers these transfers. If the information is unclear, ask about the scope of cover and any excess payable in the event of an accident.

Security at the parking facility

Your choice of parking space also matters. Outdoor parking areas should prevent unauthorised access through barriers, fences or walls. Security staff and camera surveillance can provide additional protection. To help protect your car from hail, storms and other weather, consider underground or covered parking. These spaces generally cost more but offer greater protection from the elements.

Secure storage of your car key

Only authorised staff should be able to access your key. Locked key cabinets or safes with a clear link between each key and vehicle are suitable options. Keys should be kept securely in an office, ideally away from the parking area itself.

Check your vehicle on collection

Inspect your car before leaving the meeting point. Compare the bodywork, wheels and interior with your photos, and check the mileage and fuel or battery level. If you notice any difference, tell the member of staff immediately and photograph any damage.

Can the valet service move my car?

As part of the booked valet service, your car may be driven to its parking space and moved within the site when operationally necessary. The terms should explain which journeys are included. Any unexpected additional mileage should be discussed on collection and checked against the handover report.

Optional services such as refuelling, maintenance, car washing or EV charging may involve journeys beyond the transfer between the terminal and parking space. Agree these journeys with the provider and check that the mileage is within the expected range when your car is returned.

Important: remove valuables from your vehicle

Even if the provider is reputable and the site is well secured, always remove valuables before handing over your keys. Personal belongings such as wallets, documents, jewellery, watches or cash are generally not included in the insurance cover and may not be compensated if lost.

Who is liable for damage during valet parking?

In principle, the party that causes damage and is legally responsible for it is liable. In practice, this depends on when the damage occurred and which insurance applies. If you find scratches, dents or interior damage, document them at the meeting point and obtain written confirmation that you reported them to the provider.

Frequently asked questions about valet parking safety

Will my car be parked on airport grounds?

Private valet services collect your vehicle at the terminal and then take it to an off-site parking area. The handover location therefore does not identify where your car will be parked. Check the parking location stated in your booked offer.

Are private valet services approved by Munich Airport?

Private valet services are not specifically inspected or approved by Munich Airport simply because they collect vehicles at the terminal. The provider you book is responsible for insurance, secure parking and proper delivery of the service.

Is indoor parking always safer than outdoor parking?

Covered parking offers extra protection from the weather. However, access controls, surveillance, staff and secure key storage are equally important in preventing unauthorised access. A well-secured outdoor space can therefore sometimes be a safer choice than an inadequately secured indoor facility.

When is valet parking a safe choice?

Valet parking safety does not depend on a single feature. A camera at the parking site is of limited value if the handover is not documented or it is unclear who holds your keys. Assess the whole process rather than the most prominent security claim. Choose an offer with protection appropriate to your car and your needs. A monitored indoor facility may suit a high-value or weather-sensitive vehicle, while a well-secured outdoor space with a documented handover may be sufficient for another car.
